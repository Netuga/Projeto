🌟 Guide do Iniciante

1️⃣

Primeiros Passos

▼
1

Completa o Tutorial

The tutorial gives valuable starter resources including gems and tickets. Do not skip it!

2

Faz o Reroll Inicial

Use the starter tickets to try to get at least one Legendary or higher unit. If you do not get one, recreate the account. It takes less than 5 minutes.

3

Completa Story Chapter 1 e 2

These chapters are the easiest and give enough resources for your first upgrade significativo.

4

Junta-te ao nosso Servidor do Discord

The community is essential for finding raid groups, getting tips, and tracking the current meta.

💎

How to Spend Resources

▼
💎
Gems

Guarda para invocações durante eventos com taxa de pull de lendárias aumentada. Nunca gastes em speed-ups.

🎫
Golden Tickets

Only use them to summon units that are currently meta. Always check the current tier list.

⭐
EXP

Always prioritize your main unit. One strong high-level unit beats several units médias.

🏅
Raid medals

Gasta na loja de Raid. Prioriza a Soul lendária antes dos Gears — o impacto é muito maior.

🏆

Advanced Meta Tips

▼
⚡
Speed Farming

Use Killua Storm + Lightning Soul in infinite mode for fast farming on waves 1-50.

🎯
Boss Killer

Ichigo Bankai Z with Chaos Sword and Assassin Trait is the best setup for single bosses.

🛡️
Tank in Front

Always place a tank (Erza Titan or similar) in slot 1 to absorb early boss damage.

🌊
AoE for Waves

Goku Omni or Poseidon Zero are essential for clearing fast waves in infinite mode.

💜
Support is Meta

A good support unit like Shadow Monk can double the whole team DPS with debuffs.

📅
Daily Routine

Daily quests + 3 PvP + 1 weekly raid is the minimum routine to keep resources healthy.
